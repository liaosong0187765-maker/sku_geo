# Buying Guide: Anker

## Anker 735 Charger (Nano II 65W)

A compact 65W wall charger for buyers who want one charger for supported phones, tablets, and USB-C notebooks.

### Best For

- Charging a supported USB-C notebook from a compact wall charger
- Charging up to three supported devices from one wall charger
- Replacing separate phone, tablet, and USB-C notebook chargers when 65W total output is sufficient

### Not For

- Devices that require more than 65W sustained input
- Buyers who need Samsung 45W Super Fast Charging 2.0 support

### Limitations

- Samsung S22 Ultra supports Super Fast Charging with this charger, but not 45W Super Fast Charging 2.0.
- Charging speed depends on device input limits and cable capability.
- Cables are sold separately.
